Wanderlift Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026 · Terms of Use

Wanderlift is a band and bodyweight training app for iPhone, made by Matthew Wayles ("I", "me"). It is built to work fully offline, and your training data stays on your device. This page explains the few things that do leave it.

What stays on your device

Your plans, workouts, sets, history, settings, badges and Passport stamps are stored in a database on your iPhone. I run no account system or server for this data, so I never receive it and cannot see it.

Reminders

Workout reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server, and you can turn them off in iOS Settings.

Analytics and advertising

Wanderlift currently has no product analytics, no advertising, no ad identifiers, and no tracking across other apps or websites. If this changes, I will update this page and the App Store privacy label before the change ships.

Payments

Wanderlift does not currently sell anything. If paid features are added, purchases will be handled by Apple, and I will update this page to say what that involves.

Retention and your choices

  • Your training data lives on your device. Deleting the app deletes it, and any iCloud backup is managed in your iCloud settings.
  • Crash reports are kept by Sentry for a limited period (Sentry's default is 90 days), and they are not tied to an identity, so I cannot look up reports "about you." If you want to ask about them anyway, contact me below.
  • You can deny or revoke location, Health and notification access at any time in iOS Settings.

Children

Wanderlift is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
